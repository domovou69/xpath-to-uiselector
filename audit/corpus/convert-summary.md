1645 Android literals (kinds xpath and xpath-dynamic, 140 dynamic) from 20 projects.
No device: this counts what the converter does, not whether it is right. The emulator fuzz run (stage 4b) checks
the conversions. Unique = one literal per project, occurrences = every place in the source files, project-weighted =
every project counts the same.
